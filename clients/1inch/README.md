## About 1inch

**1inch** is a leading DeFi aggregator and decentralized exchange (DEX) infrastructure provider. The 1inch Network unifies decentralized protocols whose synergy enables the most lucrative, fastest, and protected operations in the DeFi space — sourcing liquidity across hundreds of liquidity sources on multiple chains to deliver optimal swap rates to users.

The 1inch Aggregation Protocol is one of the most widely used DEX aggregators in Web3, having processed hundreds of billions of dollars in cumulative trading volume since launch. The project also includes the **1inch Fusion** mode, **Limit Order Protocol**, and the **1inch Wallet**, among other products.
