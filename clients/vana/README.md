## About Vana

Vana is a distributed network for private, user-owned data, designed to enable user-owned AI. Users own, govern, and earn from the AI models they contribute to. Developers gain access to cross-platform data to power personalized applications and train frontier AI models. The protocol introduces Data Liquidity Pools (DLPs) that allow data contributors to pool their data assets and share in the rewards generated by AI models trained on that data.

### Notable High Severity Finding (resolved)

- **[H-01]** `TeePoolImplementation#cancelJob` — Missing update of `bidAmount` after refund allowed an attacker to repeatedly call `cancelJob` and drain the contract balance. Resolved by zeroing the bid amount before transferring funds.
