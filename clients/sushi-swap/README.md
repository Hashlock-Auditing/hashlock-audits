## About SushiSwap

**SushiSwap** is a community driven decentralized exchange (DEX) and DeFi protocol operating across **35+ blockchains** including Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Base, and many more. Sushi offers automated market maker (AMM) trading, concentrated liquidity, cross chain swaps via **SushiXSwap**, lending through **Kashi**, and is recognized as one of the most established brands in decentralized finance.

Since its launch, SushiSwap has been a foundational liquidity layer for the DeFi ecosystem, with billions of dollars in cumulative trading volume and one of the broadest multi chain footprints in the industry.
